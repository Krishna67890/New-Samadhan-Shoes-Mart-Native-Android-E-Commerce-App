Privacy Policy — New Samadhan Shoe Mart

Last updated: October 18, 2026

New Samadhan Shoe Mart (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the New Samadhan Shoe Mart Android application (“App”) handles information when you use our footwear-shopping services.

1. Information We Handle

Location Information

If you choose to use the location feature, the App may request access to your device's approximate or precise location to help you identify or share your delivery address. Location information may be included in a WhatsApp message when you choose to contact us about an order.

Location access is intended for this feature. We do not claim to continuously track your location. You can deny location permission, although location-based features may not work.

Order and Contact Information

When you contact us or place an order, you may voluntarily share information such as your name, phone number, delivery address, selected products and order details. If you place an order through WhatsApp, the information you send is processed through WhatsApp and received by the business to handle your inquiry or order.

Please avoid sharing unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Device and Advertising Information

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Depending on the SDK configuration, device settings and applicable consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and advertising-related information to provide, measure, protect or improve advertising services.

2. How Information Is Used

Information handled through the App may be used to:

  • Display footwear products and related information.
  • Help customers share delivery details and communicate about orders.
  • Respond to customer inquiries and provide requested services.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve the App.
  • Protect the App and its users against misuse or fraud.

We do not state that information is collected or used for purposes that the App does not actually support.

3. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the App may request the following permissions:

  • Location: To provide a location-based address or sharing feature when requested by the customer.
  • Internet: To load online content, communicate with services and display advertisements.
  • Notifications: If enabled and implemented, to deliver relevant app notifications.

You can manage permissions through your Android device settings. Denying an optional permission may limit the related feature.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services, including:

  • Google Play services and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): To support Android functionality and advertising.
  • WhatsApp: To allow customers to contact the business and share order or delivery information when they choose to do so.

These services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Mobile Ads information: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • WhatsApp Privacy Policy: https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy

We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party services.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with third-party services when necessary to provide a feature you request, such as sending an order message through WhatsApp or displaying advertisements through AdMob.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety and security.

We do not promise that third-party services never collect or share information; their practices depend on their services and configurations.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information voluntarily sent to the business, including order inquiries and delivery details, may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the inquiry or order, maintain relevant business records, resolve disputes or comply with legal obligations.

Information processed by Google, WhatsApp or other third-party services is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

If you want to request deletion of information held by the business, contact us using the contact details provided below. We will review and respond to your request, subject to applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the business. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for its declared audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly intend to collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the business, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action. Applicable child privacy laws and Google Play requirements will be followed.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You can manage location and notification permissions in your Android settings. You can choose whether to open WhatsApp and send information to the business.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information held by the business. Contact us to exercise any applicable rights.

Where required, advertising consent and privacy choices will be provided through the applicable mechanisms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same privacy-policy URL, with a revised update date when appropriate.
